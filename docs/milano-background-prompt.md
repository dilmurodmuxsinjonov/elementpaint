## Yakuniy prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: square product image for a paint manufacturer's web catalogue. Image 1 is the exact client supplied MILANO decorative coating pail and is the edit target. Change only the environment surrounding the pail: replace the near-black dramatic studio and floor with the warm cream studio backdrop used by the Element Paint catalogue (#F5EEDF background, #EEE4D2 warm studio floor), gentle diffuse light, and a small natural contact shadow directly below the pail. Preserve the complete pail silhouette, black plastic body and lid, handle, crop, camera angle and size. Preserve the printed label artwork exactly as supplied, especially the gold crowned M, the white MILANO wordmark, the city and stone textures, all badges and icons, and the existing label text. Preserve verbatim: "MILANO", "DECORATIVE COATING", "DEKORATIV QOPLAMA", "INTERYER UCHUN", "25 KG", "MADE IN UZBEKISTAN". Do not redraw, replace, move or add packaging text or graphics. Keep the entire pail visible and centered. No extra props, products, people, background architecture, logos or watermark. Photorealistic catalogue studio lighting, square composition.
